  • Leading engineering teams

    Hiring, 1-on-1s, planning and retrospectives, and keeping a team shipping through reorganizations. About a decade of it, across two companies.

  • Agentic engineering

    Making AI coding agents productive in a real codebase with many repositories: shared context, conventions, review in CI, and knowing when not to use them.

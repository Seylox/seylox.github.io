\section*{Profile}
Engineering leader with 15+ years of building mobile apps and SDKs, 10 of them leading teams. At \textit{Anyline} I have led the same core engineering team since 2022 through several reorganizations and rounds of layoffs. Leadership kept me as team lead through each of them, and my team took on more scope every time. Today one team of five engineers handles the work of four former teams. All of Anyline's SDK and cloud development runs on the agentic engineering workflow I designed.
